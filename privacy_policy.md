\# Privacy Policy - \[Bot Xiao]



This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use information when you use \*\*\[Bot Xiao]\*\*.



\### 1. Data We Collect

We only collect minimal data necessary for the bot to function:

\* User IDs and Username.

\* Server IDs (Guild IDs).

\* Public message content (only when the bot is mentioned or commands are triggered).



\### 2. How We Use Data

\* To process commands and provide bot features.

\* To maintain server-specific settings (e.g., prefix, welcome messages).

\* We \*\*do not\*\* sell your data to third parties.



\### 3. Data Retention

\* Data is stored as long as the bot is in your server. 

\* If you kick the bot, your server data will be deleted from our database within 30 days (or upon request).



\### 4. Third-Party Services

We may use Discord's API to provide our services. Please refer to Discord’s Privacy Policy for more information.
